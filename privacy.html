Kube Roll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Kube Roll ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Game progress

Your progress (completed levels, stars, diamonds, purchased cube colors and styles, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game, Android may restore it from your device backup; otherwise it is lost.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are started, completed or abandoned, how many moves they take, and whether hints were used. This helps us improve level difficulty and fix problems.

Firebase automatically collects some information about your device and app usage, such as device model, operating system version, app version, approximate location (country or city, derived from your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored), session times, and an app-instance identifier. On devices where it is available and not limited by you, it may also collect the Android Advertising ID. This information is used to measure how players find and use the game, including the results of our advertising campaigns.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal content. The game has no accounts and no chat.

Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Analytics data is processed by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We may use aggregated statistics (for example, "40% of players finished level 10") with our advertising partners, such as Google Ads.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, contact us at the address below and include the date you installed the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
